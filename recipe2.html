BBQ Chicken Pizza

Prep: 15 mins | Cook: 20 mins | Serves: 3 | Level: Medium

What You Need

  • Pizza crust
  • BBQ sauce
  • Shredded chicken
  • Red onion, sliced
  • Shredded mozzarella
  • Chopped cilantro

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  2. Spread BBQ sauce on crust.
  3. Add chicken, onion, cheese.
  4. Bake ~20 mins. Top with cilantro and eat!
