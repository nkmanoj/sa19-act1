Margherita Pizza

Prep: 10 mins | Cook: 15 mins | Serves: 2 | Level: Easy

What You Need

  • 1 pizza dough (store-bought works!)
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • Fresh mozzarella slices
  • Fresh basil
  • Olive oil, just a drizzle

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 475°F.
  2. Roll out dough and spread sauce.
  3. Add mozzarella and bake for ~15 mins.
  4. Top with basil and olive oil. Done!
